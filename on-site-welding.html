On-Site Welding Across Washington State

Nearly all of Coal Tech LLC's work is performed on-site — at your facility, your job site, or your commercial property. We bring the equipment to you, set up safely alongside your team, and complete the work without disrupting your operations more than necessary.

For commercial clients, this means zero transport costs, no downtime waiting for parts to come back from a shop, and a welder who's actually familiar with working in active commercial environments — around other trades, under time pressure, and to code.

On-Site Welding Work We Perform

🚪 Door & Frame Welding

Hollow metal door and frame repair performed directly at your building. No need to remove frames — we weld in place.

🏗️ Structural Welding

Structural steel repair, bracket installation, beam work, and structural reinforcement — performed on-site at your commercial or industrial facility.

⚙️ Equipment Repair

Industrial equipment welding and repair performed in-place. We minimize downtime by fixing equipment where it stands rather than transporting it.

🔒 Security & Access

Gates, barriers, security frames, and access hardware — welded and repaired on-site. Particularly common after break-ins or vandalism.

🏭 Facility Maintenance

Ongoing facility welding support for maintenance teams — handrails, platforms, supports, storage systems, and structural fixes as they come up.
