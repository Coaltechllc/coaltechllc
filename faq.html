Services Our Welding Services
What types of welding does Coal Tech LLC perform? +
Coal Tech LLC performs all major commercial welding processes: MIG (GMAW), TIG (GTAW), Stick (SMAW), and Flux-Core (FCAW). We work with aluminum, stainless steel, and carbon steel for commercial and industrial applications across Washington State.
Do you provide on-site welding services? +
Yes. All services are performed on-site at your location throughout Washington State. We bring fully equipped mobile welding units directly to your job site — no need to transport materials or equipment. Whether it's a commercial facility, active construction site, or secure installation, we come to you.
Do you fabricate and repair hollow metal doors and frames? +
Yes — this is one of our core specialties. Coal Tech LLC fabricates and repairs hollow metal doors and frames, including fire-rated openings and high-security commercial facilities. Do you offer welding training and workforce upskilling? +
Yes. Coal Tech LLC provides hands-on welding skill refresher and workforce upskilling programs for corporate and commercial clients.
