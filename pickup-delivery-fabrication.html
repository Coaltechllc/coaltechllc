Commercial Metal Fabrication with Pickup & Delivery

Not every welding job can be done on-site. When you need custom fabrication — parts, frames, supports, specialty hardware — Coal Tech LLC offers a full pickup and delivery service throughout Washington State. We come to you, collect your materials or specs, fabricate in our facility, and return the finished work to your door.

This service is built for contractors, property managers, and facility teams who don't have the time or transport capability to move heavy steel to a shop and wait around for it.
